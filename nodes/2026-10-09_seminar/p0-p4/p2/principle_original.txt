- **IF** a noun phrase in a definitional, characterization, or causal/etiologic context denotes an **abnormal clinical or pathological state**—even when its head is a generic state/process/symptom noun rather than a canonical disease label—**THEN** annotate it as **DiseaseClass**; **do not apply this rule** when the phrase refers only to a normal biological process, a molecular/substance entity or level, or an isolated descriptive finding that is **not** presented as a pathological condition of the patient/disorder.
